Our Story

This website was made with the intention of contecting booklovers with their next exciting adventure through the pages. What started as a passion project brought on by two young coders has developed into a wonderful website for all to enjoy and hopefully find their next read. Given our background knowledge of retail and marketing when involving books we hope that this website is able to connect you with your next big read.

As fellow booklovers ourselves we know that these systems tend to connect you with books not based on similar vibes or reader experience but by taglines and author descriptors, which are not alwasy accurate, Our webiste focuses mostly on fellow user recommendations and tagline descriptors to connect you with your next read.

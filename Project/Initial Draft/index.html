The Paris Novel

Ruth Reichl

The Paris Novel tells the story of Stella St. Vincent, a quiet copy editor from New York whose life takes an unexpected turn when her estranged mother leaves her a ticket to Paris. Hesitant but curious, Stella steps into the City of Light and finds herself swept into a world of vintage fashion, mysterious art, and unforgettable meals. With the help of a wise old art collector and a community of bohemian book lovers, Stella uncovers long-lost secrets and begins to rediscover herself. A heartfelt journey of healing and transformation, this novel celebrates the magic of Paris and the courage it takes to rewrite your own story.

Howl's Moving Castle

Diana Wynne Jones

Howl's Moving Castle follows a quiet hat-maker named Sophie, who’s cursed by a jealous witch and transformed into an old woman. Seeking answers, she stumbles into the magical, walking castle of the mysterious and flamboyant wizard Howl. As Sophie navigates enchanted rooms, fire demons, and flying battles, she discovers her own strength and unravels the secrets behind Howl’s heart—literally. A whimsical tale of magic, identity, and love, where courage blooms in unexpected places and appearances are never quite what they seem.

A Bold Pumpkin Plan

Katy Hudson

A Bold Pumpkin Plan tells the story of a small shy hedgehog looking to build a new home out of his gourd of choice, a big pumpkin. The hedghog meets lots of helpful critters along his way to deliver his new home and learns that maybe new friends aren't so bad. A touching children's story with adventure about overcoming one's fears yet staying true to themselves.

Iron Widow

Xiran Jay Zhao

Iron Widow tells the story of Wu Zetian, a fierce teenage girl in a futuristic world where giant mechas defend humanity—but at a deadly cost. Girls are sacrificed as psychic fuel for male pilots, and Zetian volunteers not to serve, but to seek revenge for her sister’s death. When she survives the cockpit and kills her co-pilot, she’s branded an Iron Widow—a rare and feared force. Teaming up with the notorious Li Shimin and navigating a tangled love triangle, Zetian battles alien invaders and the brutal patriarchy that feeds on young girls. A fiery sci-fi adventure about reclaiming power, rewriting destiny, and refusing to be silenced.

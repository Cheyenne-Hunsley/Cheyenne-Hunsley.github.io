Portfolio

Here is a protfolio of my past and current ongoing Projects.

Bookaneer
A book review website

For this project, my partner and I, made a website for readers who wish to share their own books in like a personal library style.
The website allows readers to add certain books using an ISBN database to their "Libraries" and leave comments and rate the Book itself along with recommended other books.

Mystery Falls
A text-based mystery game.

The choose your own adventure style of this game allows the player to enter a name and stats and investigate a Manor to solve a Murder.
Future plans include additional stat increases and implimenting mulitple story endings and different victim outcomes.
